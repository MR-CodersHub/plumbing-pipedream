1. Information We Collect

When you book a plumbing service, submit inquiries, or request an instant cost estimate, we collect personal and property information necessary to perform technician dispatches.

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, physical service site address.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Property type, diagnostic issue details, photos attached during booking.
  • Preferences: Dark/light mode settings and LTR/RTL layout preferences saved in your browser's localStorage.

2. How We Use Data

Your information is used exclusively to deliver fast, accurate plumbing services and maintain high quality standards.

  • Dispatching mobile technician units to your service location within 30-45 minutes.
  • Generating flat-rate binding price quotes and itemized digital receipts.
  • Sending SMS notifications regarding technician ETA status.

3. Data Sharing Rules

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Data is shared strictly with authorized dispatch personnel and secure payment processors.

4. Security Measures

All data transmitted through our online booking forms and dispatch portal is protected with 256-bit SSL encryption and strict database access controls.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request a copy of your service records or request complete deletion of your customer profile at any time.
