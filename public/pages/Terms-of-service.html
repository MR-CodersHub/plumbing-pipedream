1. Dispatch & Booking Tickets

By submitting an online booking order or confirming a dispatch over the phone, you authorize Plumpin master plumbers to dispatch a mobile utility unit to your specified address.

  • Cancellation Policy: You may cancel standard scheduled visits without fee up to 60 minutes prior to technician arrival.
  • 24/7 Priority Emergency Dispatch: Emergency requests (<45 min response SLA) incur a standard emergency dispatch fee disclosed prior to truck departure.

2. Upfront Flat-Rate Pricing

We operate strictly under upfront flat-rate pricing. You will receive a written estimate before any wrench is turned. No work begins until you approve the binding rate.

3. Wall Cutouts & Site Access

If repairing in-wall pipes requires drywall or tile removal, our technicians cut precise diagnostic access panels. Drywall patching, painting, or tile restoration is the responsibility of the property owner unless explicitly included in the work order.

4. Workmanship Warranties

All repairs and installations executed by Plumpin are backed by our written warranty terms:

  • Drain Jetting: 30-day clog-free warranty.
  • Water Heater Installs: 10-year manufacturer tank warranty + 1-year labor warranty.
  • Whole House Repiping: 25-year structural pipe guarantee.

5. Liability & Governing Law

Plumpin maintains full commercial liability insurance ($2,000,000 policy) and state master plumber certification (#9401). These terms are governed by state plumbing board codes.
