Emergency & Dispatch Queries

What is your average response time for emergency dispatches?

Our average emergency response time is 30 to 45 minutes from ticket confirmation. Priority emergency units are on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Can I schedule a specific time window for non-emergencies?

Yes! You can choose 2-hour arrival windows on our Online Booking Page (e.g. 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM) and receive a 30-minute advance text callout prior to technician arrival.

Pricing & Binding Quotes

Are your prices hourly or flat rate?

We provide binding flat-rate quotes upfront after on-site diagnostic inspection. You know the exact total before any repair starts, so there are never surprises if a job takes extra time.

What payment methods do technicians accept?

All mobile technicians carry wireless POS terminals accepting major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfers, and checks.
